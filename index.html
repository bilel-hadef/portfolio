Who I am

About Me

I'm a BI & Data Analytics Engineer with 5+ years of experience building end-to-end data and analytics solutions using the Microsoft ecosystem, including Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, and Azure, alongside tools such as dbt, Airflow, Terraform, and Azure DevOps.

I take a pragmatic, business-first approach to technology: before designing a solution, I make sure I understand the problem we're actually trying to solve. From optimizing ETL processes and modernizing data warehouses to improving platform governance and solving performance issues, I enjoy turning complex data challenges into solutions that are reliable, scalable, and useful to the business.

I'm comfortable working across the full data lifecycle, from architecture and engineering to analytics and BI, and while I enjoy collaborating and advising, I'm at my best when I can get hands-on and build things myself. My goal is simple: use technology to solve real business problems and create measurable value.
